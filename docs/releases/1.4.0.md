Leading now runs on macOS 14 (Sonoma) and macOS 15 (Sequoia) as well as macOS 26, on Apple Silicon and Intel. Nothing else about it changes.

- **macOS 14 and later.** Everything works on the older systems: vaults, embeds and link previews, highlights, math, diagrams and code, print, and the Quick Look preview.
- **No stray focus ring.** On macOS 14 and 15 a new window no longer opens with a blue ring round the note's Properties.
