Leading 1.7 searches the whole vault, and lists links to a note at once.

- **Search the vault.** Edit › Find › Search Vault… (⇧⌘F) opens a Search tab in the margin. Type, and every note in the vault holding your words is listed, with the lines where they are. Click one to open it at that line, with Find on the words. Case, accents and curly quotes are ignored, as in Find.
- **Links to this note, at once.** Leading now keeps an index of the vault in memory while one of its notes is open, and follows changes made in other apps. The Links tab, wikilinks and embeds answer from it instead of reading the vault each time, and the Links tab updates as other notes change.
- **No false links.** Where two notes share a name, a `[[Name]]` link is now listed only for the note it opens.
- **Says what went wrong.** A link to a note, heading or file that is not there, a link in a note not yet saved, and Highlight or Look Up with nothing selected now explain themselves in a short notice instead of a beep. Wikilinks to notes your vault lacks are dimmed.
- **Links for other apps.** `obsidian://`, `zotero://` and other apps' links open in the app that handles them.
- **Menus show where things stand.** View › Theme picks a theme by name, Next Theme (⌥⌘T) includes Auto again, the margin's tabs are ticked in the menus while showing, and size and width commands grey out at their limits. Reading commands work while Settings or Aa is in front.
- **The margin from the keyboard.** ← and → move between the margin's tabs, ↑ and ↓ through each list, and ⌥⌘L puts you in the highlights. VoiceOver hears each highlight's colour. The outline's filter opens folded sections round a match, and Esc clears it before closing the outline. A note in the Links tab opens at the line that links here.
- **Easier to read.** The outline's text is darker on every paper. With Increase Contrast on, the margin stays at full strength and Focus dims less; with Reduce Motion on, a linked passage is marked without the fade.
- **Aa, tidied.** The reading aids carry their menu names, and Aa gains Smart Punctuation, how deep the outline goes (H1–H2 to H1–H4), and Restore Defaults.
- **Windows come back as they were.** After a restart, a window returns in edit mode or plain text if it was, with the margin on the same tab. The Dock icon offers New Note.
