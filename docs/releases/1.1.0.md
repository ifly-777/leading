Leading now remembers where you were, and keeps you on the page.

- **Your place in every file.** Close a file and open it again, even weeks later, and it opens where you stopped reading.
- **Footnotes beside the text.** Click a footnote number and the note appears just under the line, with no jump to the end and back. Esc or a click elsewhere closes it; ⌥-click goes to the notes at the end, as before.
- **Links between notes.** `[[Wikilinks]]` now open the note they name, found the way Obsidian finds it: beside the note, then anywhere in the vault. `[[Note#Heading]]` and `other.md#heading` open at that heading. View › Back (⌘[) returns from a jump within a document.
- **Reading progress, if you want it.** View › Show Reading Progress (⌥⌘P), or the Aa panel, draws a hairline under the title bar that fills as you read, and shows the time left while you scroll. It is off unless you turn it on.
