# Notes on *Walden*

> [!quote] Why he went
> I went to the woods because I wished to live deliberately, to front only the essential facts of life.

Thoreau’s case for a simple life is mostly arithmetic. He prices his house to the half-cent, and the total is the argument: <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">a man is rich in proportion to the number of things which he can afford to let alone</mark>.

## Lines to keep

- <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Our life is frittered away by detail.</mark>
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Time is but the stream I go a-fishing in.</mark>
- ==To affect the quality of the day, that is the highest of arts.==
