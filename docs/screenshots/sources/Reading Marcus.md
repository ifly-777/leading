# Reading Marcus

He wrote for himself, not for us, which is why the ==Meditations read like a pilot's notes to his future self==: short, practical, repeated until they stick.

## The morning

The famous opening of the second book, kept here as he wrote it:

![[Meditations#Book II]]

<mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Expect difficult people, and do your work anyway.</mark> That is the whole instruction, and it is enough for a day.

## Retreat

He keeps returning to one idea: <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">the quietest place available is your own mind</mark>, and it is always open. See [[Meditations#Book IV]].
